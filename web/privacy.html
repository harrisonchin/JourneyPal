Privacy Policy for JourneyPal Pro

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

JourneyPal Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with activity and travel tracking features, JourneyPal Pro may access or process the following data on your device:

  • Location Data: Access to your device's GPS location to display and log your activity points on interactive map views.
  • Photos and Camera: Access to your camera and photo library so you can attach photos to your journal and activity entries.
  • Local Storage: Activity entries, dates, and locations are saved locally on your device using encrypted database persistence.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely for internal app functionality, including:

  • Displaying your journal timeline and map logs.
  • Organizing your activities by date, location, and photos.
  • Ensuring off-line access to your personal activity records.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties. All journey entries and media files remain on your device or within your private account ecosystem unless you explicitly share them.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is saved locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can modify or delete your entries at any time directly within the app, or erase all app data by clearing local storage or uninstalling the application.

5. Security

We value your trust in using JourneyPal Pro. We implement standard security practices and local encryption protocols to safeguard your activity data.
